## Context

- SF-43 is `awaiting_review` on PR #87 (source-bearing audited head `fd3741bd`; metadata head `4691b1d1`). Its confirmatory results are
  known (`docs/experiments/2026-11-03-sf43-confirmatory-macrodispersion.md`): 7 of 8 Holm hypotheses rejected; `gate5:4` not established
  (Fieller 90 % [1.159, 1.228], descriptive verdict `disagree`); pre-registered decomposition at `sigma^2 = 4`: consumer/Pollock 1.021
  [0.987, 1.056], Pollock/Par2 1.164 [1.131, 1.198] (Student intervals of `mcstat.gate5.gate5_paired`), so the offset is on the Par2 side
  with its mechanism (Euler step, horizon truncation of the slow tail, injection) not separable with the frozen data.
- A post-hoc reading (owner session of 2026-11-04, unaudited; it is verified in this addendum, not accepted from its summary) found:
  - the G7 generator effect `alpha_TP` 1.48 [1.11, 2.10] at `sigma^2 = 1` uses the POSITION basis (the `alpha_TP` column without suffix,
    `sf43ana/loader.py`), because the pre-registration did not fix the G7 basis; in the primary displacement basis it is about 1.05 with
    an interval containing 1 (recorded as deviation D8, recomputed officially in the post-hoc section N1);
  - at `sigma^2 = 4` the Pollock/Par2 ratio grows with the fraction of Par2 particles that did not cross the last window plane before
    Par2's horizon (selection, not causal), which points to censoring of the slow tail, with a residual of about 5 % that the horizon does
    not explain;
  - the far/main plateau of `alpha_TP` up to 194 `lambda` rests only on the width 24 `lambda` (arm `s1_dist`).
- No change of C++/CUDA or of the runners is needed: the SF-43 executor already supports the Par2 options `hz` and `dt_half` and the
  consumer geometries of J3 and J9. New ANALYSIS scripts are needed (they compute `alpha` contrasts), so the new head is source-bearing
  and needs human review (`docs/runbooks/autonomy-policy.md`).
- The frozen `plan.json` points to `~/sf43_campaign` (budget 150 GPU-h, cutoff 2026-11-08T19:00Z); running it again would write next to
  the raw arrays needed by acceptance F. A new plan with a new campaign directory is needed. `sf43plan.build.check_seeds` rejects
  overlaps, so the paired reuse of seeds must be declared.

## Decision

1. **Scope (accepted).** Addendum 1 of SF-43 adds, outside the Holm family and as descriptive evidence only:
   - **J1** (Par2 at `sigma^2 = 4`, horizon against step): arms `j1_base10`, `j1_H3`, `j1_D`, `j1_DH3`, `j1_H2` on seeds of `s4_g5p`,
     paired with `s4_g5p` and with the frozen Pollock twin `s4_g5pol`;
   - **J3** (`lambda/h = 5` twin of `s1_dist`, paired by seed);
   - **J9** (width 48 `lambda` at 208 `lambda`, `lambda/h = 5`, 150 new seeds);
   - **S0** (smoke, structure and cost only);
   - **J8** (conditional extension of J1 by 40 seeds), as a second short job after the J1 analysis, only under its pre-registered
     trigger (owner answer above).
   Not authorized (owner): the 832 `lambda` box, `sigma^2 = 0.0625`, the generator contrast with Par2 and any tracker run on the
   random-spectral generator.
2. **Rules (accepted).** Fixed in the addendum pre-registration (`preregistration-addendum-1.md` and `prereg_addendum.json` of the
   SF-43 artifact) before any script of the addendum is written and before any run: the arms (exact copies of their origin arms but for
   the manipulated factor), the seeds (paired reuse declared; new disjoint block for J9), the estimators, the inference method, the
   classification rules of J1, J3 and J9 (the owner's text verbatim in its Appendix A; the operational version may only tighten it, each
   tightening listed), the smoke cut rules, the J8 trigger, the budget and the cutoff. The J1 classification, applied in order, first
   match wins:

   | Order | Class | Condition (point estimates are ratios of means over common seeds; IC90 as pre-registered) |
   |---|---|---|
   | 1 | `horizonte dominante` | `r_H >= 1.10`, `abs(r_D - 1) <= 0.03` and IC90(`q_H3`) within [1/1.10, 1.10] |
   | 2 | `paso dominante` | `r_D >= 1.05` and `r_H < 1.05` |
   | 3 | `mixto` | `r_H >= 1.03`, `r_D >= 1.03` and IC90(`q_DH3`) within [1/1.10, 1.10] |
   | 4 | `abierto` | any other case, or lower limit of IC90(`q_DH3`) above 1.10 |

   with `r_H = Par2(H3)/Par2(s4_g5p)`, `r_D = Par2(D)/Par2(s4_g5p)`, `q_X = Pollock(s4_g5pol)/Par2(X)`; `q_H3` and `q_DH3` are always
   reported with their intervals as the residual, which the rule does not attribute. The reproduction check and the manipulation check
   gate it (pre-registration).
3. **Budget, cutoff, disk (accepted).** Hard cap 8 GPU-h for the whole addendum, S0 and J8 included (SF-43 total at most 61.7 of the
   150 of G10); one GPU, one remote job at a time; no unit runs after 2026-11-07T03:00Z (`cutoff_utc + grace_minutes` at most that
   instant); campaign `~/sf43_addendum` (smoke `~/sf43_addendum_smoke`), peak at most 60 GB, enforced by the engine's disk guard;
   `~/sf43_campaign` read-only; `$HOME/sf39_campaign`, `$HOME/sf42_campaign` and `~/sf43_artifact_stage` untouched; any deletion
   goes to the owner first.
4. **Post hoc analyses (accepted as post hoc, labelled).** N1 (G7 in the primary basis: the frozen `sf43ana` with a copy of the
   pre-registration that only changes `g7.responses`; deviation D8), N3 (positive control by plane), N5 (`alpha_LP` budget table and the
   `lambda/h` 16 vs 32 paired ratio at `sigma^2 = 4`), N6 (local `alpha_T` against distance), N8 (Par2 completeness strata at
   `sigma^2 = 4`, selection not causal), optional N7 (backflow proxy). They are reported in a separate, labelled section of the note
   and never change a pre-registered result.
5. **Acceptance F modality (owner decision 4).** The independent reduction of at least three retained realizations is PREPARED BY AN
   AGENT (a script that imports no project module and implements the definitions of the pre-registration section 4 from the raw arrays,
   with a one-page runbook) and EXECUTED AND COMPARED BY THE OWNER. This is weaker than a reduction written by the human.
6. **Procedure (accepted).** This record, the addendum pre-registration, the specification amendment and a bitácora row are committed
   and pushed to PR #87 (no force-push) before any script, check or run of the addendum. Scripts follow worker -> orchestrator audit ->
   integration, are frozen by sha256 in a bitácora row and synced before the smoke. The blinded phase (workflow 8A) covers S0, the
   production job and J8, with a freeze row (sha256) before any addendum result is read. SF-43 is `active` while executing and returns to
   `awaiting_review` on PR #87 with a new source-bearing head; the human approval must name that head. No agent merges.

## Consequences

- **Post-hoc additions, disclosed.** The arms were defined after the frozen results were known. Mitigation: every rule is fixed before
  its first run, the arms are exact copies of frozen arms but for one factor, and the addendum is descriptive and outside the family. It
  does not change any confirmatory decision; acceptance D stays the owner's decision, with J1 as input.
- **Paired reuse of seeds.** J1 reuses seeds 434001-434060 of `s4_g5p` with its transport seeds (common random numbers: `j1_H3` repeats
  the first `3.6 (L1 - 10 ell)/dt` steps of `s4_g5p` exactly and continues them); J3 reuses 436001-436100 of `s1_dist`. Their contrasts
  are paired and correlated with the frozen arms by design.
- **Scope limits.** J1 can attribute the offset between horizon and step only within its design (the injection factor is not varied at
  `sigma^2 = 4`). J3/J9 speak about the far/main ratio at `lambda/h = 5` and width 48 `lambda` at 208 `lambda` only; no claim about
  Lester's asymptotic statement.
- **Proposed for owner ratification (not decided here).** Erratum of the specification's class rule at `sigma^2 = 0.25`
  (specification implementation item 3, lines 118-119: `physical (restricted)` "at `sigma^2` 1, 2.25, 4") against the pre-registration
  (section 6, line 287: "possible at all four amplitudes, H4") and decision H4 (`m = 8` with `sigma^2 = 0.25` in the family): the
  pre-registration governs; the specification text predates H4.
- **Cost.** At most 8 GPU-h; review of a larger PR.
