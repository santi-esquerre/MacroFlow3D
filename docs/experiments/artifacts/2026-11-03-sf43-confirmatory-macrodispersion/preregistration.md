## 4. Estimands [G4, H2; spec 1]

Reducer `sf42red` of SF-42 with the disclosed SF-43 additions (`--pollock`, `--keep-arrays`, `--single-pass-hmax`) [spec "Files"]. Window
planes `d` in `[d_lo, d_hi]` (at least 3) from the injection plane `x0 = 5 ell`; OLS slopes against `x1`; `alpha` in `lambda` (divided by
`ell`); moments centred per realization, BIASED weighted form with weights renormalized over the particle set; within-realization OLS
standard errors are never used [G4].

- `alpha_TP = (1/2) slope[(var x2 + var x3)/2]/ell` (fixed axes). `alpha_TAP`: the same on the transverse variance orthogonal to the
  realization's mean displacement `q` (first to last window plane; Gram-Schmidt of `e2`, `e3` against `q/|q|`). `alpha_LP = (U_r^2/2)
  slope[var tau]/ell`, `U_r = 1/slope[mean tau]`, `tau` the time since injection; the ensemble estimand is the MEAN of the per-realization
  values (the `(Ubar^2/2) x mean slope` form is reported beside it) [G4].
- **Basis** [H2]: PRIMARY = displacement `x23(d) - x23(seed)` (`disp`); the position basis (`pos`) is a secondary from the same runs;
  `alpha_LP` is basis-independent.
- **Weights and sets**: primary flux weights, set `perplane` (particles that crossed that plane); check set `common` (crossed every window
  plane); `unif` weights for Gate 5 and Par2 (Par2 rows: `unif`, `perplane`, from the plane-crossing CSV); `expI` (`flux *
  exp(-planes_I)`) is a weight-sensitivity check only, never a comparator [G table, spec 1]. Basis contrasts [H2; block `basis` of
  `sf43ana`, descriptive]: per amplitude on the production analysis set, the paired difference `pos - disp` of `alpha_TP` and
  `alpha_TAP` with flux weights (mean, 90 % Student and bootstrap-t intervals; ratio of means), and the same contrast on the
  uniform-weight copy of the set (`<amp>_produnif`), the exact control (expectation 0 by transverse stationarity); an interval
  excluding 0 is a flag that indicts the estimand pipeline or the stationarity, not the physics.
- **Windows** [spec 1, H17]: `main` [25, 94] (primary, every arm); halves `h1` [25, 59.5], `h2` [59.5, 94] (production arms), reported as
  intervals, no claim (no equivalence margin is pre-registered); `far` [94, 194] on the distance arms with the ratio far/main.
- **Secondaries** (descriptive, outside the family) [spec 1, 9, H12]: SF-39-style time-window estimators `alpha_LAM`, `alpha_Tfixed`,
  `alpha_TAM` (window `[0.75, 1] t_end,r` ending at each realization's first exit) on production, Gate 5 Par2 and distance arms; the
  B&dD-style window (their eq. 6 averaged over it, Table 1 parameters); `exp(-I)` weights; Par2's transverse dispersivity in the matched
  configuration (both bases) and its log-ratio to the consumer twin; the split-half (checkerboard) particle share of every estimator.
- **References** [H3, H17]: the unbounded first-order window value `7.992e-5 sigma^2 lambda` (= `c`) and the box first-order values
  (`firstorder_box.py`: -3.5e-5 `sigma^2 lambda` at W = 24, L = 104; -4.1e-6 at W = 12), per arm; the positive control (section 7).

## 5. Hypotheses, family and decision rules

5.1 **Family (fixed, m = 8)** [H4, G5.6]: `robust:s025`, `robust:s1`, `robust:s225`, `robust:s4`, `gate5:s025`, `gate5:s1`, `gate5:s225`,
`gate5:s4`. Holm step-down at a family-wise 5 % (at most 6.25 % with G5.3's 1.25 tolerance): step `i` uses the level `0.05/(8 - i + 1)`
(0.00625, 0.05/7, ..., 0.05); the first non-rejection stops. A hypothesis that cannot be tested (below a floor (5.4), missing input, not
qualified at the realized sizes (5.4), no qualified Gate 5 procedure, fewer than 3 pairs, non-positive mean) enters with `p = 1` and is
never rejected; `m` stays 8 [H4]. An exception, or a non-finite p-value, while computing one family hypothesis makes that hypothesis
untestable in the same way (`p = 1`, status `error`, recorded in `process.errors`; Holm runs over the fixed `m = 8`) [C-ROBUST;
`sf43ana/README.md`; section 14, R11].
Decisions are taken AT the Holm level (`RL(level) > c`; Gate 5 `p <= level`), ties with relative tolerance 1e-9 (a tie is not a
rejection); lattice and Holm-adjusted p-values are reported [H4].

5.2 **Robust sign** [G5.4, H3, H6-H8]. For `e` in {`alpha_TP`, `alpha_TAP`} (displacement basis) and amplitude `a`, null `alpha_e <= c =
7.992e-5 sigma^2 lambda` (1.998e-5, 7.992e-5, 1.7982e-4, 3.1968e-4 at 0.25, 1, 2.25, 4): reject iff `RL_e(a) > c`, `RL_e(a) = L_e(a/2) -
sum_{k=1..K} B_k(a/(2K))`; the data are never shifted [H3]. `L_e` = the one-sided BOOTSTRAP-t lower limit of the production mean (`B =
33599`) [H15]; each `B_k` is made non-increasing in its level (suffix maximum on the lattice); bootstraps resample jointly every arm
sharing seeds [G5.4]. The amplitude hypothesis needs both estimators to reject (intersection-union); `p_e = inf{a : RL_e(a) > c}` on the
lattice, amplitude `p = max(p_TP, p_TAP)` [G5.4]. Components: K = 3 at 0.25 and 1 (`E_h`, `E_W`, `E_int`), K = 4 at 2.25 and 4 (plus
`E_WxH`).

- `E_h` [H6]: ladders paired by seed with production: `lambda/h` 5/10/20 (0.25, 1; production in the middle), 4/8/16 (2.25; production
  finest), 4/8/16/32 (4; production finest). Order of a constant-ratio triple: `p = log(|m_j - m_i|/|m_k - m_j|)/log r` (monotone only; a
  non-monotone or oscillating resample has no real order and counts as outside); `p_L` its lower limit; range test `[0.5, 2.1]` on EVERY
  triple; `p_use = min(p_L over the triples, 2)`. Finer branch (production not finest): `1.25 UCB|a(h_p/r) - a(h_p)| r^p_use/(r^p_use -
  1)`; finest branch: `1.25 max(UCB|a_P - a_{P-1}|, 2^-p_use UCB|a_{P-1} - a_{P-2}|)/(2^p_use - 1)` (Giles 2015); otherwise the fallback
  `3 UCB(max_ij |a_i - a_j|)/(r_min - 1)` on the ratio-2 levels. **Gate on the asymptotic range** (order branch only): at 2.25 the
  `lambda/h = 12` level (`s225_c12`, same seeds) must agree with the 4/8/16 law (90 % paired-bootstrap interval of `a(12) - a_fit(12)`,
  `a_fit = a_inf + C h^p` on the triple, contains 0); at 4 the orders of 4/8/16 and 8/16/32 must agree (90 % interval of their difference
  contains 0); a failed gate gives `E_h = max(order branch, fallback)`. Upper bounds `U` are Student-studentized differences of per-seed
  values.
- `E_W` [H7, G5.4]: `UCB|a(W*) - a(W_prod)|` at the finest measured `lambda/h`, widths UNPAIRED: at 0.25 and 1 `s*_w48` against `s*_prod`
  (48 vs 24, `lambda/h` 10); at 2.25 `s225_w48_12` against `s225_c12` (48 vs 24, 12); at 4 `s4_w24_16` against `s4_l16` (24 vs 12, 16).
  `E_WxH` (2.25 and 4): `UCB|[a(W*,h_f) - a(W*,h_c)] - [a(W_prod,h_f) - a(W_prod,h_c)]|`, `h_c` = 8 (`s225_w48_8`/`s225_eh8`;
  `s4_w24_8`/`s4_l8`). Stated assumptions: the transfer to the production `lambda/h` (12 to 16 at 2.25, 16 to 32 at 4), the residual
  beyond the widest width, the outlet boundary layer (about `W/2 pi`) inside the 5-`lambda` buffer [H7].
- `E_int` [H8, G5.4]: `2 UCB|a(h/16) - a(h/8)|` from the paired passes of the first 30 production seeds (order 1 assumed); Student
  contrast bound. Each `B_k` is taken at `a/(2K)`; a bound built from two estimated inputs takes each at half its level [G5.4].

5.3 **Gate 5** [G5.5, H5]. Estimand `mu_L/mu_P` of the plane-based `alpha_LP` (consumer twin, uniform weights; Par2), paired by seed,
ratio of arithmetic means. TOST in Fieller form: one-sample tests on `D_hi = L_r - 1.10 P_r` (reject for small mean) and `D_lo = L_r -
P_r/1.10` (reject for large mean), each at the Holm level, `p_tost = max(p_hi, p_lo)`. Pairs = the first `n` positions of the two arms'
analysis sets (seed orders agree; stops at the first non-finite pair); `n` = floor = 200 / 200 / 200 / 250 at 0.25 / 1 / 2.25 / 4.
**Procedure** = the first of (i) Hall (1992) skewness-corrected t (Student reference), (ii) bootstrap-t at the level (`B = 33599`), (iii)
bootstrap-t at half the level, that the committed coverage summary (section 11) qualifies at the LARGEST coverage grid point `<=` the
realized `n` (all pairs are used) under `normal`, `stress`, `stress-`, both sides, at every tail used, and at `sigma^2 = 4` also under the
J3B-bound family (skewness 2.73, excess kurtosis 7.59, both signs); none qualified: descriptive, `p = 1` [H5, H15]. The 90 % ratio
interval is obtained by inverting the tests. Reported beside it (descriptive): the Student Fieller form, the SF-42 delta-method TOST,
achieved power (Fieller form at the realized `n`, observed SD of `D_hi`, `D_lo` and their correlation, at ratios 1, 0.95 and the observed
one), the pair-exclusion sensitivity (pairs with a `fallback` verdict in either code removed) [H5] and the SF-39-style unmatched
comparison [spec 7].

5.4 **Floors** [H13]: Gate 4 at an amplitude needs production >= 100; ladder >= 30 complete PAIRED seeds (common prefix of every ladder
arm, the gate arm and the production-width arm of the group); every width cell >= 30; `h_max` passes >= 20 paired seeds; plus each arm's
own `floor` (`prereg.json`; 20 for the control); Gate 5 the pairs of 5.3. Below a floor: untestable, `p = 1`, estimates descriptive.

**Qualification at the realized sizes** [H4, H13, H15; C-GAPS; `sf43ana/qualification.py`]. The floors are necessary, not sufficient. The
robust sign at an amplitude is testable only if (i) its floors hold, (ii) the pipeline of its structure `K` is qualified (every cell of the
profile passed) at the LARGEST coverage profile whose every arm size (production, paired ladder, smallest width cell, `h_max` pairs) is at
most the realized one, and (iii) the bootstrap-t sampling limit is qualified at the largest grid `n` in {100, 150, 200} `<=` the realized
`N`. Profiles: `full` = variant A sizes; `half` = production 100, ladder 50 (`K` = 3) / 75 (`K` = 4), width 75, `h_max` 20. Otherwise
`p = 1`, status `not qualified at the realized sizes` (also without a document: `no qualification document`; an incomplete document,
`all_cells_complete` not true, qualifies nothing); estimates, budgets and classes stay descriptive. Stated assumption (not tested):
qualification at a smaller size carries over to larger sizes (so only the largest eligible profile is consulted, and `n` above 200 after
a re-estimation is covered by the `n` = 200 evidence).

5.5 **Bootstrap settings**: seed 20261103 in every stream; `B_sampling` 33599 (exact quantile ranks for every Holm level `0.05/j` and its
half) [H5]; 4000 for the budget bounds and the class limit, 5000 for BCa (reported only, never a verdict) [G5.3; section 14 (e)]; a tail `t`
is resolvable only if `t (B + 1) >= 1` (reported by `process.bootstrap_resolution`).

## 6. Classes [G5.7, H16] and Gate 5 verdicts [G5.5]

Per transverse estimator and amplitude, in order of precedence: `physical (restricted)` = Holm rejects the amplitude's robust-sign
hypothesis (possible at all four amplitudes, H4); `numerical` = the production two-sided 90 % bootstrap-t interval lies above `c` AND the
one-sided 95 % upper limit of the bias-corrected estimate is below 0.5 x the production mean (bias-corrected = production mean + signed
Richardson correction, finer branch `(a(h_p/r) - a(h_p)) r^p/(r^p - 1)`, finest `(a(h_p) - a(r h_p))/(r^p - 1)`, `p = p_use` if in range
else 1, + signed width correction `a(W*) - a(W_prod)`; upper limit by a seed bootstrap resampling jointly the arms sharing seeds);
`unresolved (bounded)` = otherwise, reporting `alpha_T <= U(0.05) + sum_k B_k(0.05/K)`; `unresolved` = anything else, including different
classes for `alpha_TP` and `alpha_TAP`. `numerical` and the bounds are descriptive. Every class is qualified "within this model, medium,
width range and travel window, under the stated transfer assumptions" [spec 3]. Only an `E_h` or `E_int` artifact would support Lester et
al.'s attribution of `alpha_T` to numerics; a width artifact would not [H16].

**Gate 5** [G5.5, H5]: the CONFIRMATORY outcome is the Holm decision of `gate5:<a>` (`p_tost`, Holm level). The verdict `agree` (90 %
Fieller interval of the ratio inside [1/1.10, 1.10]) / `disagree` (entirely outside) / `inconclusive` is DESCRIPTIVE and never decides the
hypothesis. A `disagree` at any amplitude is outside the family, triggers the attribution (consumer-twin / Pollock-twin / Par2
decomposition at every amplitude; G7 at 0.25 and 1) and, per the specification, keeps SF-43 active until attributed or decided by the
owner [spec acceptance D, G5.5]. Pre-registered expectation [H5]: at `sigma^2 = 4` the plane-based `alpha_LP` is a slow-tail, non-Fickian
quantity and a `disagree` is plausible.

## 8. Schedule and process [H13, H14; engine `sf43camp`]

- **Order and lanes.** Priority class, then progress fraction of the CRN group on `n_conf`, group order, anchor seed, replacements, arm
  order; all arms of a group advance together. Before each claim a lane reads the free memory of its GPU. Lane A (first granted GPU)
  prefers the largest `min_free_mib`; lane B (second GPU) takes the half-class units (`lane_b_min_free_mib` 17000) in queue order, takes a
  unit outside its class only when memory allows at once (never waits for one), logs `LANE_B_RELEASED` and leaves when none of its class
  remains, when its next unit would end after `stage1_start + 30 h` (nothing is killed), or when a third-party compute process appears on
  its GPU (section 1.4). One GPU granted (the GPU policy of 1.4, or after the 7200 s wait with `REMOTE_GPU_COUNT_MIN=1`) or a resume after
  the release: lane A only. Planning [H9]: two-GPU stage 12-16 h, 47-63 h of wall (60-79 h on one GPU, the planning case). The H14
  exception ends with stage 1 and in any case on 2026-11-09.
- **Cutoff and guards.** Cutoff `2026-11-08T19:00:00Z` (16:00 -03): no new claim; in-flight units may finish until cutoff + 60 min, then
  they are terminated and recorded with the seeds found complete; then the frozen analysis [H13]. Budget guard 150 GPU-h (used = GPUs held
  x wall; a unit is claimed only if used + in-flight + expected <= budget; else STOPPED, exit 11) [G10]. Disk guard 100 GB free (reduced
  segments are pruned; else STOPPED). CPU-only slot-hours (the coverage run) are not counted toward G10 and are reported separately [H15,
  owner decisions of 2026-11-01].
- **Failures.** OOM is not a failure: the unit's remaining seeds wait 10 min, doubling to 60 min per consecutive OOM of that unit.
  `config_error` halts the arm; `other_error` retries once, then halts it. An arm halts when failed/attempted exceeds `halt_rate` 0.25
  with at least 20 attempts (a systematic-failure detector, not a quality gate: expected rates in section 7); a halted arm stops and goes
  to the owner [spec 5].
- **Blinded variance re-estimation** [G5.2, H9]: for the 10 arms of `blinded_reestimation.arms` (production and wide `E_W` cells; never a
  Gate 5 arm, a ladder rung or a distance arm), once `0.25 n_conf` realizations are reduced the engine reads standard deviations and a
  count only, never a mean: per estimator `N_req = ceil((t_{0.95,n-1} sd_UB80/hw)^2)`, `sd_UB80 = sd sqrt((n-1)/chi2_{0.2,n-1})`, `hw` the
  spec-item-6 half-width target (section 7, both transverse estimators); `n_target = min(floor(2 n_conf), max(n_conf, max_e N_req), n_max,
  seeds)`, never below `n_conf`; the extension seeds are priority-2 units. Variance only, upward only, at most 2x, once; inert unless the
  SD is several times the planning value [H9]. The coverage evidence of the production sampling limit is at `n` in {100, 150, 200}; an
  arm re-sized above 200 uses the `n` = 200 evidence under the stated assumption of 5.4 (section 11; section 14, R10).
- **Reductions**: a background thread; a failed one is retried 3 times (back-off 1, 5, 15 min); every unreduced segment is re-submitted
  before the analysis; what stays unreduced is MISSING and listed.
- **Relaunch.** Helper exit 75 (slot busy, no eligible GPU): nothing ran; resubmit the same command under a NEW job name. A dead or
  cancelled job, or a deliberate relaunch, continues with `all --resume` under a new name (the ledger is replayed; units of a dead
  launcher restart at their first incomplete seed). Past the stage-1 cap, or when only one GPU is granted, the resubmission asks for one
  GPU (`REMOTE_GPU_COUNT=1`; `RESUME_SINGLE_LANE`) [H14, engine]. Exit codes: 0 DONE, 10 DONE_PARTIAL, 11 STOPPED, 12 analysis failed,
  13 refused to start, 143 cancelled; the analysis runs after the loop except after a refused start or a signal.

## 12. Interpretation statements [H17]

No asymptotic claim (Lester et al.'s `t -> infinity` claim is out of scope). The estimand is the first-passage distribution at each plane
(recrossings counted and reported). `alpha_LP` at 4, and partly at 2.25, is a non-Fickian, pre-asymptotic travel-time quantity, compared
with B&dD only through their eq. 6 at `t = d/U`; `alpha_TP` is compared with B&dD's `alpha_TAM` as an order of magnitude only. First-order
references are the box values per arm beside the unbounded ones. The halves and the distance window separate "decay toward zero" (first
order, Lester 2021's illustrative eq. 5.43) from "plateau" (B&dD, or numerical drift), not physics from numerics. A positive `alpha_T` is
not presupposed physical. W8/G9a and G9b are recorded, not tested. Model restrictions [decision G, "Model restrictions"]: Gaussian
covariance, scalar isotropic `K`, medium (b), SF-18 periodic normalized generator (periodic along `x` with the box length), purely
advective transport, periodic transverse boundaries.

## 13. Deviations policy [spec acceptance A; workflow 8A]

Every deviation from this text is recorded in the bitacora with its reason and date; no threshold, margin, level, family member, floor,
estimator or rule is changed after data. The pre-registration is pushed to the draft PR before the first run, with no force-push;
amendments are new commits that say what changed and why. Infrastructure interruptions (exit 75 or 70, ssh drop, OOM from a colleague's
process, host reboot) are resumed with identical settings and disclosed; a scientific outcome is never re-run with changed settings. Dry
runs and the smoke test are not evidence. GPU-h per job are reported (stage 1 at 2 x wall; the ledger records the granted list and when
the second lane exited). An in-job analysis failure (exit 12, marker `ANALYSIS_FAILED`) is a defect, recorded as a deviation; the same
frozen command is re-run once, as a CPU job on the same inputs, after an environment-only fix (recorded); any code change after data is a
recorded deviation, flagged in the experiment note, and the result of the frozen command stays primary (section 14, R12).
