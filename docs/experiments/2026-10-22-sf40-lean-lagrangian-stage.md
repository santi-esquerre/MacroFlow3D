## Question

The increment's exact Goal: `Bajar el pico de memoria de dispositivo del camino lagrangiano (campo SF-18, flujo SF-19, splines SF-28, tracker) con un stage liviano paramétrico en (nx, ny, nz) para que la caja de 3328x384x384 celdas (4.9e8; hoy caben 1.5e8) corra en una V100-32GB con pico medido <= 30000 MiB, con salidas del tracker idénticas byte a byte al camino actual, speedups bit a bit del tracker y sin buffers muertos en el baseline Par2.`

Claims allowed (SPEC): (a) the lean path reproduces the current path's outputs byte for byte on the cube; (b) the exact per-phase device bytes
equal the closed form of level S+; (c) whether 3328x384x384 fits in 30000 MiB (measured); (d) the non-cubic stage is correct on exact controls;
(e) bitwise tracker speedups (measured, no threshold); (f) the Par2 `FlowWorkspace` is 24 B/cell smaller with byte-identical outputs.
Not allowed: any `alpha` estimate, any change of numerical semantics, any claim about `lambda/h` (SF-37 owns it).

## Caveats

- **One realization per D size** (512^3 `d512`; 3328 two fields at different amplitudes, seed 4001). The PID peaks are the maximum of one run each; run-to-run variation of the unmodeled residual (0.73 and 11.45 MiB observed)
  is not characterized. The 1115 MiB margin to 30000 MiB is a measured margin of those runs.
- **Sampler resolution 200 ms**: it can miss transients shorter than 200 ms; it did miss the cuFFT peaks of S1 and S3 (not resolved at any run), and sampled the F peak only as a single sample. The D verdict rests on
  the S2 plateau (584-588 samples); the S1/S3 peaks are bounded by the exact-byte report (S2 exceeds S3 by 5989774977 B and S1 by 10129031785 B at `d3328_i`), not by sampling.
- **Unmodeled overhead measured at two sizes only** (0.73 MiB at 512^3, 11.45 MiB at 3328): its growth law is unknown; the feasibility table holds `U` at its 3328 value. The ~138-140 MiB above T exact + C during
  tracking appears at all three sizes and is below the S2 plateau by 20572 MiB at 3328 (28885 - 8313, derived).
- **Host RSS** is a `Maximum resident set size` of the process (40 B/cell above the probe, agreeing with the enumerated host set); host RAM headroom is not a limit at 4.9e8 cells (19.8 GB used of 134.6 GB).
- **The S2 closed form counts the scratch actually allocated by this build**: the `cub` temp sizes (`blas::sum_workspace_bytes`) depend on the CUB version of CUDA 11.4.152; another toolkit changes `S_scr` (133225 B)
  by a few KiB, not the structure.
- **Completeness instrument**: for F/S1/S3 it does not see the cuFFT plan internals (disclosed in C).
- **The audit's CompactMAC excess** (+1/nx) was an underestimate for this geometry (+1/nz), 0.0833 B/cell (+40.9 MB at 3328); it is part of the pre-registered closed form and was measured exactly.
- **No `alpha` estimate is made or implied** (Rule 1; SPEC "Not allowed"). The `TPLANE` / `TTIME` dispersion lines of the D runs are not reported or interpreted; the `n_ok` counts are quoted only to state how many
  particles were traced. F4 compares plane statistics only as a relative-difference control between two representations of the same field. No claim about `lambda/h` beyond restating SF-37's pairs.
- The J1b dry run and the J0a dry part are not evidence; only their job logs are imported.
